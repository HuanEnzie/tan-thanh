Character portrait sheet for a video game set in a zombie post-apocalypse city. Paint exactly 12 DIFFERENT FEMALE characters as head-and-shoulders bust portraits, arranged in a grid of 4 columns x 3 rows. Each portrait is its own square painting with a thin dark border, all the same size, separated from each other by wide gaps of flat solid magenta (#ff00ff) background. Use exactly the same illustration style, painterly pixel-art-anime rendering, dramatic rim lighting and level of detail as the two reference portraits (study their faces, eyes, shading and background treatment). The 12 characters are a mix of unusual and memorable characters: some elders aged 50 to 70, some with eyepatch / mask / facial paint / heterochromia / dramatic hair colors, plus one or two teenagers aged 15 to 17. They must look clearly like 12 different people: varied face shapes, noses, eyes, expressions (smiling, serious, smirking, weary, fierce, gentle), hairstyles, hair colors and skin tones, ages. Wardrobe and background fit the world: survivors in worn modern clothes: hoodies, patched military jackets, medic coats, mechanic overalls, scarves, gas masks hanging at the neck, bandages, backpack straps, goggles, dog tags; ragged but characterful; dusty ruined-city or burning-street blurred background in warm orange and dark teal. Characters face slightly toward the viewer, eyes visible. No text, no captions, no watermark, no weapons covering faces.
